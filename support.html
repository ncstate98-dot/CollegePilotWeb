CollegePilot Plus

Restore purchases from Profile using the same Apple App Store or Google Play account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your store account settings. Deleting your CollegePilot account does not cancel store billing.

AI features

AI features require adult-parent consent, an active CollegePilot Plus subscription, and an internet connection. The free planner remains available without AI.

Planning data

Your plan is stored on your device. Sign-in does not synchronize plans between devices. Use Profile to export or import a backup when moving planning data. Protect exported files because they may contain private family information.

Reminders

Enable notifications in CollegePilot and in your device settings. Reminders are scheduled locally and can be affected by permissions, device settings, time zones, or operating-system limits.

Privacy and account actions

Use Profile to revoke AI consent, clear local planning data, or request account deletion. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for more information.

When contacting support

Include your CollegePilot version, device and operating-system version, and a brief description of the problem. Do not email your child's sensitive records, credentials, or complete plan unless a secure support process has been agreed.
