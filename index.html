Who CollegePilot is for

CollegePilot is for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. Provide information about a child only when you are authorized to do so. Avoid unnecessary personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your college plan is stored on your device. It may include a profile, college list, applications, notes, dates, progress, reminders, and saved AI results. Signing in does not synchronize your plan between devices. You may export a readable backup to a location you choose.

Optional AI planning

AI features require adult-parent consent and an active CollegePilot Plus subscription. When you request matches, advice, or deadline research, relevant planning information is sent through Firebase to Google Gemini. This may include grade, academic information, interests, location preferences, budget expectations, colleges, application status, dates, and completed tasks.

CollegePilot removes a child's name, exact home city or ZIP code, private notes, religion, household income and assets, and local student ID from structured AI requests. Other free-text fields may still contain personal information, so avoid entering unnecessary sensitive details.

Accounts and purchases

Anonymous Firebase access and optional Apple or Google sign-in may process account identifiers and authentication information. Firebase stores service-request records, consent version and timestamp, hashed request IDs, request locks, and monthly AI usage counts. RevenueCat processes purchase-related information and an app-user identifier to determine subscription access. Apple or Google handles store payments; CollegePilot does not receive full payment-card details.

Device features

Notifications are optional and scheduled locally. Backup import accesses only a file you select. This version does not request contacts, precise location, photos, camera, microphone, health, advertising tracking, or advertising identifiers.

Service providers and retention

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where Firebase, Google, RevenueCat, Apple, or Google operate. Local planning data remains on your device until you clear it or delete the app. Account and usage records are retained as needed for service operation, subscription enforcement, abuse prevention, deletion processing, and legal or provider obligations. Each provider's own current terms govern its separate retention practices.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke AI consent, clear local planning data, export a backup, or request account deletion from Profile. Deleting an account does not cancel a store subscription. Copies you export or share are outside CollegePilot's control.
